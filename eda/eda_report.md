# Enterprise Workforce Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report

## Executive Summary
Comprehensive analysis conducted on 650 active enterprise employee records across 7 organizational divisions.

### Key Analytical Insights
#### Department Distribution
- **Finding:** Engineering and Data & AI constitute 49.5% of total enterprise headcount.
- *Largest Department:* Engineering
- *Largest Count:* 175
- *Largest Pct:* 26.9
- *Smallest Department:* Customer Support
- *Smallest Count:* 55
- *Total Departments:* 7

#### Gender Distribution
- **Finding:** Female representation stands at 40.5%, demonstrating strong gender diversity balance across technical and business functions.
- *Female Percentage:* 40.5
- *Male Percentage:* 56.6
- *Non Binary Percentage:* 2.9

#### Experience Analysis
- **Finding:** The workforce has an average experience of 7.5 years, with a healthy distribution from entry-level apprentices to veteran technical leads.
- *Mean Experience:* 7.5
- *Median Experience:* 6.7
- *Min Experience:* 1.0
- *Max Experience:* 20.8

#### Salary Analysis
- **Finding:** Engineering commands the highest median compensation ($135,300), reflecting high market premiums for deep engineering and AI specialization.
- *Highest Paying Dept:* Engineering
- *Highest Paying Median:* 135300.0
- *Overall Median Salary:* 115800.0
- *Overall Mean Salary:* 119385.0

#### Top Technical Skills
- **Finding:** AWS (129 practitioners) and SQL (116 practitioners) form the organizational foundational technology stack.
- *Top Skill:* AWS
- *Top Skill Count:* 129

#### Department Performance
- **Finding:** Product leads company performance with an average appraisal score of 3.73 / 5.0.
- *Highest Performing Dept:* Product
- *Highest Avg Rating:* 3.73
- *Overall Avg Rating:* 3.67

#### Skill Gap Analysis
- **Finding:** Sales & Marketing displays lower average technical skill inventory (4.4 skills/person), highlighting prime candidates for targeted cross-functional upskilling.
- *Highest Skill Density:* Customer Support
- *Highest Avg Skills:* 5.1
- *Lowest Skill Density:* Sales & Marketing
- *Lowest Avg Skills:* 4.4

#### Exp Vs Perf
- **Finding:** Correlation between experience and performance rating is 0.04, demonstrating that high performance occurs across both newer agile talent and tenured leaders.
- *Correlation Coefficient:* 0.039

#### Training Impact
- **Finding:** Employees who completed corporate certifications achieve a +0.01 higher average performance appraisal score compared to untrained peers.
- *Trained Avg Performance:* 3.67
- *Untrained Avg Performance:* 3.66
- *Performance Lift:* 0.01

#### Correlation Findings
- **Finding:** Job Satisfaction (-0.06) and Work-Life Balance (-0.07) show strong negative correlations with Attrition, signaling retention sensitivity to workplace well-being.
- *Salary Experience Corr:* 0.88
- *Satisfaction Attrition Corr:* -0.06
- *Balance Attrition Corr:* -0.07
